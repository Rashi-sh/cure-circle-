# 🩺 CureCircle  
### *Explore. Understand. Choose the Right Treatment.*

---

## 🌟 Overview  

CureCircle is a healthcare platform that helps users explore different types of medical pathies and connect with doctors to choose the most suitable treatment.

Instead of limiting users to one medical system, CureCircle provides awareness, comparison, and guidance across multiple treatment approaches.

---

## 🎯 Problem  

- Lack of awareness about different medical pathies  
- Confusion in choosing the right treatment  
- Limited access to doctors across different systems  
- Scattered and unreliable information  

---

## 💡 Solution  

CureCircle brings all medical pathies into one platform, allowing users to:

- Learn about different treatment systems  
- Compare approaches  
- Connect with relevant doctors  
- Make informed healthcare decisions  

---

## 🩺 Supported Pathies  

- Allopathy  
- Ayurveda  
- Homeopathy  
- Naturopathy  
- Unani  

---

## 🚀 Features  

### 👤 User Panel  
- Explore different pathies  
- Learn treatment methods  
- Book consultations  
- View doctor profiles  
- Track appointments  

### 🧑‍⚕️ Doctor Panel  
- Manage patient interactions  
- View appointments  
- Maintain patient history  
- Provide treatment guidance  

### 🛠️ System Features  
- Multi-pathies platform  
- Structured healthcare information  
- Clean and responsive UI  
- Scalable architecture  

---
